# Senior Living Growth Strategy and Financial Forecast

2024 Bain Cup, Team Nautilus. Pengyu Chen, Haifei Yang, Qinxin Luo and Hao Luo developed a growth strategy for Taikang Senior Living & Care. The team modeled occupancy, resident fees and operating costs for a senior living community.
